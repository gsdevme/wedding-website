Room Bookings

To book a room at the venue, please contact Christine Fox via phone 01866 833333 or email christine@argyllhospitality.co.uk. Please note that there is no fee for booking and cancellations (fingers crossed won't be required!) are free.

How to get there

From Glasgow, follow the A82 past Loch Lomond to Tyndrum. At Tyndrum, take the A85 towards Oban. Just before you reach Taynuilt, turn left and take the B845 to Kilchrenan. Turn left at Kilchrenan Inn and follow the road until you reach Ardanaiseig. Heads-up: the B845 onwards is a single-track road.
